Delete your Energy Today data

Energy Today by Kea Today (Rotchana Dixon) · Google Play: com.kea.energytoday

How to ask us to delete your data: wipe the app on your phone, then email kea@kea.today with the subject “Energy Today delete my data”.

Steps

  1. Open Energy Today on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Clear All Data and confirm. This removes your name, birthday, journal, how you feel, voice notes, Health Connect imports, diet photos, and scores stored on that phone.
  4. Or uninstall Energy Today. That also removes the on-phone copy.
  5. Email kea@kea.today from the address you used with us. Ask us to delete the Energy Today records we hold. Say your first name if you remember it.

What is deleted

  • On your phone: name, date of birth, journal, check-ins, optional Health Connect import (heart, HRV, steps, exercise, distance), optional diet photos, optional voice notes, and calculated scores — as soon as you clear data or uninstall.
  • On our servers (after your email): AI / coach messages, the app-open log (hashed id and first name), and any email you sent us about the app.

What we cannot delete for you

  • Google Play purchases. Pro payments stay with Google Play. Manage or request those through your Google account.
  • Health Connect / Google Fit / Apple Health. Those copies live in those apps. Turn off Energy Today access there if you want.
  • Weather services (ip-api, Open-Meteo) only see a rough location at the moment of a weather fetch. We do not keep a location file on you.

How long we keep what left the phone

  • On-phone data: until you clear it or uninstall. It is not deleted after 90 days on its own.
  • AI messages and the app-open log: until you email us, or as long as needed to run the service.
  • After a delete email we aim to remove our copy within 30 days.
