Privacy Policy for Energy Today

Last updated: 8 September 2026

In short: Your name, birthday, journal, and optional Health Connect import stay on your phone. If you use AI, weather, or we record that you opened the app, a small amount of data goes to our Energy Today servers or weather services. Google Play handles Pro payments. We do not sell your data. This is not a medical app.

Who we are

Energy Today (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is a daily check-in and timing app from Kea Today. Package name on Google Play: com.kea.energytoday.

This policy covers the Energy Today mobile app and the related pages on kea.today.

Not a medical device

Energy Today is for reflection and planning. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or predict health, jobs, or money. Numbers in the app are about how the day fits you, not a medical measurement.

What stays on your phone

These stay in the app on your device unless you choose to send them (for example by using AI or export):

  • Name and date of birth — so Today can speak to you
  • How you feel, journal, and optional voice notes
  • Energy scores and history calculated on the device
  • Optional Health Connect import (see below)
  • Optional diet photos or barcode scans you attach in Pro health tools

You can delete this on the phone: Settings → Clear All Data, or uninstall the app.

Health Connect (optional)

Only if you tap Import. We do not pull health data in the background.

With your permission, Android Health Connect may share the last 30 days of:

  • Heart rate
  • Resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Steps
  • Exercise sessions
  • Distance

We use that so you can see how your body sits next to today’s energy number. It stays on the phone. We do not sell it. We do not use it for ads, employment, or insurance. You can turn off access in Health Connect or Android Settings.

On iOS, the same idea applies if you later allow Apple Health.

What can leave the phone

AI / coach boxes

If you type into an AI or insight box, that text (and the context needed to answer) is sent to our Energy Today backend so a reply can come back. Hosted at energy-today-backend-4.onrender.com. We do not use those messages for ads.

App open log

When you reach Today with a profile, the app may send a hashed id (from name + birthday) and your first name to the same backend, so we can see that the app is being used. It does not send your full birthday or journal.

Weather (optional)

Today and some Pro rooms may fetch weather. If you grant location, we use a city-level GPS point. If you do not, we may use a rough location from your IP (ip-api.com) and weather from Open-Meteo. We do not sell location.

Google Play purchases

If you buy Pro, Google Play processes the payment. We receive that you purchased. We do not see your full card number.

Google Calendar (optional)

Only if you connect Calendar. Then event titles and times you choose to sync go through our backend to Google’s Calendar API.

Website and email

If you write to kea@kea.today or hello@kea.today, or buy a letter on kea.today, that email and the details you typed are used to reply and deliver what you asked for.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal or health data
  • We do not use Health Connect data for advertising
  • We do not use health data for employment or insurance decisions
  • We do not run advertising networks inside the Energy Today app

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Notifications — optional reminders
  • Health Connect — optional import, types listed above
  • Location — optional, for weather
  • Camera / photos — optional, Pro diet tools
  • Microphone — optional voice notes

You can refuse any of these and still use Today.

How long we keep what left the phone

  • On-device data: until you clear it or uninstall
  • AI messages on the backend: only as long as needed to answer and keep the service working
  • App-open log: a short operational record (hashed id + first name)
  • Play purchases: kept by Google Play under Google’s terms

To ask us to delete what we hold on the backend, email kea@kea.today from the address you used, and clear data in Settings as well.

Children

Energy Today is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.

International

Servers may be outside your country (including the United States). If you use AI, weather, or the app-open log, that data may cross borders. On-device data does not leave until you use those features.

Changes

We will update the date at the top of this page when the policy changes. The live page is always https://www.kea.today/privacy-policy.html.
