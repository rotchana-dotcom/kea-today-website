Your Personal Timing Optimization Tool

Energy Today analyzes your personal energy patterns based on your birth date and provides daily insights to help you make better decisions about when to take action, when to rest, and when to push forward.

Whether you're planning a business launch, scheduling an important meeting, or deciding when to start a new project, Energy Today gives you the clarity you need to optimize your timing.

Key Features

📊

Daily Energy Score

Get your personalized energy score each day to understand your optimal timing for important activities.

📅

7-Day & 30-Day Forecasts

Plan ahead with energy forecasts showing your best and most challenging days for the coming weeks.

✨

Personal Energy Profile

Discover your unique energy patterns, life themes, and recurring cycles based on your birth date.

Smart Journal Templates

Activity Recommendations
